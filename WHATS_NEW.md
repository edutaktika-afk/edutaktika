
**Benefits:**
- ✅ **Cleaner Interface** - No information overload

- ✅ **Faster Loading** - Only active tab loads data
- ✅ **Better Focus** - Work on one thing at a time
- ✅ **Mobile Friendly** - Responsive tab design
